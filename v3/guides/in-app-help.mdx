The round **Help** button with the robot sits at the bottom right of every
page. Open it any time; it doesn't leave the page you're on.

## When something goes wrong

When the app refuses an action, for example a co-signature that can't go
through or a wallet that can't be linked, the helper picks the matching
fix. A red dot on the button means a problem is waiting. Open it to see:

- what happened, in plain words
- why it happened
- the steps that fix it, with a link to the screen that does each one

When the fix has steps to take in order, click **Walk me through it**. The
helper shows one step at a time, with **Next** and **Back**, and keeps your
place if you leave the page. **See all steps** shows the whole list, and
**Done** on the last step closes it.

## Find a fix yourself

With no problem on screen, the **Fix a problem** tab lists every fix by
topic:

| Topic | Covers |
| --- | --- |
| Co-signing | settlement keys, which key an order needs, and when it's too late to sign |
| Wallets and sign-in | linking, unlinking and moving a wallet |
| Pods | hosted pods and their keys |
| Orders and ads | orders, deposits and your ads |
| Disputes | filing and answering a dispute |
| Connection | when the app can't reach ProofBridge |

## FAQ

The **FAQ** tab answers a few common questions: why a cancel has a waiting
window, what can end a dispute, which kill switch to use on an agent, and
where you should never sign your identity message.

If the helper doesn't cover it, [report it](/v3/guides/report-a-bug).
