A **hosted pod** is an agent that ProofBridge runs for you. Hosted pods
run on infrastructure ProofBridge operates, and each request needs a
ProofBridge operator's approval before anything runs. The pod creates its
own agent key inside the pod; only the public half leaves it. The key is
limited by the on-chain policy you set. One pod can trade for several of
your ads.

You need an active ad to request a pod.

## The steps

```
Request → Pod → Ad → Policy → Key → Emergency stop → Live
```

The setup page follows your pod through each step and updates on its own.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request">
    On **My Agents → New Agent**, choose **Hosted pod**. Enter the agent
    name, a contact email, the chains the pod's key is registered on, and
    the RPC endpoints. Choose whether the endpoints are **Kept with the
    pod** (stored encrypted, restored after a server loss) or **Held by
    you** (never stored; you re-enter them after a server loss). **Use
    public endpoint** fills in a public one. **Check endpoints** tries
    each one from ProofBridge and says whether it answered on the right
    network; it's advice only, so you can send the request either way.
    No wallet signature is needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Under review">
    An operator reviews the request, usually within a day. If the pod
    server is full, the request joins the waiting line. If it's declined,
    you see the operator's note and can **Request again**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Starting your pod">
    Once approved, the pod starts. This takes a few minutes. If it fails
    to start, **Try again** redeploys the approved request.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose an ad">
    Pick the ad the pod trades for and click **Assign this ad**. The ad
    must be held by your [agent account](/v3/guides/linked-accounts). If
    it's your wallet's, the page shows **Move this ad to your agent
    account**: create the account if you need one, then move the ad. Its
    free liquidity comes back to your wallet and the same ad opens from
    your agent account, assigned to this pod.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the agent's policy">
    Set the most per order, the most per day, and how long it lasts. Your
    wallet writes the policy on your agent account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Register the pod's key and its emergency stops">
    Click **Approve key registration** and sign once to register the
    pod's key under your ad's settlement identity. Then click **Sign the
    pod's emergency stop**. ProofBridge keeps the stop and sends it,
    without you online, if the pod is revoked from your agent account or
    in an incident. If your own key is on the same wallet, sign one for it
    too (**Sign your key's emergency stop**): when the stops are sent, your
    own key on that wallet ends as well.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fund your agent's gas">
    The pod pays the network fee for every lock from its own accounts on
    each chain. Top them up from the page; on Stellar, **Use Friendbot**
    creates and funds the account on testnet. See
    [Gas for a hosted pod](/v3/guides/faucet#gas-for-a-hosted-pod) for where
    to get test ETH. ProofBridge emails your contact address if a balance
    runs low.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go live">
    When the pod, key, ad and emergency stop are ready, click **Go live**.
    The pod starts locking within its policy.
  </Step>
</Steps>

ProofBridge also emails your contact address when the request is
approved or declined, when the pod goes live, and if it fails to start.

## Several ads on one pod

A pod trades for up to 16 ads on one network, held by the same agent
account, settled under the same wallet and locking the same token. They
share the policy's limits. On the pod's **Overview** tab, the **Ads**
list shows how many it holds. Choose one under **Add an ad** and click
**Add ad**; an ad that can't join is greyed out. **Remove** takes one
out.

If the live policy doesn't cover an ad yet, the pod can't trade for it:
the page says so and offers **Sign a new policy**. Taking out the last
ad, or changing a pod's ads in a way that drops its policy link, waits
until the open trades on that account have ended.

## The Pod tag

An ad a live pod trades for shows a **Pod** tag on the bridge page, on
**My Ads** and on its orders. The pod signs for it without waiting for
the maker.

## Withdraw the pod's gas

On the pod's **Machine** tab, **Withdraw gas** sends what's left in the
pod's accounts back to your wallets. Choose where each chain's gas goes,
sign once with the owner wallet, and the pod sends it when it next
checks in, within a minute or two.

## Limits

- Only makers can request a pod: you need an active ad, from your wallet
  or a linked account.
- Each maker can run a limited number of pods (two by default).

## Removing a pod

The agent page has three ways out:

- **Stop the pod** deletes the pod. It stops signing and its data goes.
  Its key stays registered until you retire it.
- **Retire the key** stops the pod's key on every chain.
- **Kill Agent** does both: the key is retired on every chain (one wallet
  signature), then the pod is deleted.

Before you confirm, the sheet counts the open trades on the pod's key:

| Row | What happens to those trades |
| --- | --- |
| **You take over after** | the pod locked them but hasn't co-signed; you move each one to your own key from its trade page |
| **Finish as they are** | the pod already co-signed; each completes when the Bridger signs |
| **End through cancel** | claimed or disputed already; each ends at its deadline |

## Take over a deleted pod's trades

Once the pod is deleted, each trade it locked but hadn't co-signed shows
a take-over panel on its trade page: "Your pod was deleted before it
co-signed this trade."

1. Click **Take over**. Your wallet signs once; the relayer pays the gas
   for both chain calls.
2. The order moves from the pod's key to your own settlement key on the
   ad chain. The Bridger is asked to sign again once it lands.
3. When the page says the trade is on your settlement key, co-sign it as
   usual.

The panel shows the last moment you can take it over. If your settlement
account has no key of its own live on both chains, click **Register a
key** first. If the try fails, click **Try again**. If the last moment
passes, the trade ends through the cancel path at its deadline. See
[Take over a deleted pod's trades](/v3/api/trades#take-over-a-deleted-pods-trades)
for the API.
