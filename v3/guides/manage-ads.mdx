**My Ads** lists every ad you've created, including ads held by your
linked agent accounts, with its route, available liquidity and limits.

## Tabs

| Tab | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **All Ads** | everything |
| **Active** | ads serving trades |
| **Pending** | ads whose creation transaction hasn't landed yet |
| **Inactive** | paused or exhausted ads |
| **Completed** | closed ads |

## Actions

Open an ad's menu:

- **Reactivate:** shown on a paused ad. A paused ad keeps its funds but
  isn't on the bridge page and takes no new orders; this puts it back
- **Top Up:** add liquidity
- **Withdraw:** take out liquidity that no open trade is holding
- **Close Ad:** reclaim the remaining liquidity. Close only once the ad's
  open trades have ended.
- **Complete Setup:** finish an ad whose creation didn't complete

Top Up, Withdraw and Close Ad are transactions from your wallet; the app
confirms each one landed. An ad a hosted pod trades for shows a **Pod**
tag.

<Note>
**Available vs liquidity:** a new order can take your on-chain liquidity
minus what open trades hold. Reservations from orders that are never
funded are released after ~30 minutes.
</Note>

If your agent account has a **guard rail**, large withdrawals and closing
are scheduled and wait the delay you chose. See
[Agents](/v3/concepts/agents#guard-rails).
