ProofBridge works with one wallet per chain:

- **EVM** (Sepolia): MetaMask, Rainbow and other browser wallets, or
  WalletConnect
- **Stellar** (Testnet): xBull, Freighter, Lobstr, Hana, or WalletConnect

Click **Connect Wallet** to open the wallet hub. It lists each network
and whether you're connected and signed in there.

## On a phone

An EVM wallet works on a phone through WalletConnect or your wallet's own
browser.

For Stellar, Freighter, Lobstr and Hana's browser versions are desktop
extensions. On a phone, the app to try is **Freighter Mobile** over
**WalletConnect**: it supports message signing, but it hasn't been
verified with ProofBridge yet. Set the app to **Testnet** before you scan
the code, or it declines the pairing. The Lobstr app pairs, but it can't
sign the message a settlement key needs, so you can't register a key
through it.

## Which wallets do you need?

The wallet of the chain **you deposit from** is required. Connecting both
is recommended:

- as a **Bridger**, you deposit on the order chain and receive on the
  other chain
- as a **Maker**, your liquidity lives on the ad chain and you receive on
  the order chain

## Sign in

Connecting asks for a one-time sign-in signature: a challenge, with no
transaction and no gas. EVM wallets sign a Sign-In with Ethereum message;
Stellar wallets sign a SEP-10 challenge.

Connecting your second wallet links it to the same account, so the app
shows all your trades in one place. The hub shows **Link wallet
required** until you do.

### Unlink or replace a wallet

To swap a wallet, click **Unlink** on its row in the hub. Your other
wallet signs to confirm, so you need it connected. Unlinking signs you
out on every device, and any agent accounts linked through that wallet
are unlinked too. Trades already open still pay out to the receive
address each was opened with, and only the unlinked wallet can close the
ads it made. You can't unlink your only wallet.

If you connect a new wallet while the old one is still on the account,
the row says **Another wallet is linked**: unlink the old one first.

### Move a wallet from another account

A wallet that once signed in on its own has its own account. When you
connect it to link, the hub says "This wallet is on another account" and
offers **Move it here**. The wallet signs once, and its ads, trades,
settlement keys, faucet claims and past notifications move with it. The
old account's email, push devices and settings stay behind, and you
can't sign in to that account again. An account that holds a second
wallet, a pod or an agent account can't be moved from.

You stay signed in while you use the app. After two weeks without use, or
30 days in all, you sign in again.

<Note>
Each trading wallet also needs a one-time
[settlement key registration](/v3/guides/register-a-settlement-key)
before it can complete trades. The app prompts you when it's needed, or
you can do it up front on the **BLS Keys** page.
</Note>
