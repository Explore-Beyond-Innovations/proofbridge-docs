Soak runs on testnets, so you need test funds on both sides.

## In-app faucet

The **Faucet** page mints the test tokens the soak routes use (WXLM and PB
on Sepolia, wETH and PB on Stellar) to your connected wallet.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Faucet">
    Each network has its own section. Connect the wallet for that network
    if it shows **Not connected**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Claim a token">
    Click the token to claim. The faucet mints **1,000,000** of that token
    to your wallet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for it to land">
    The claim shows **Processing**, then **Confirming**, then
    **Confirmed**. It usually takes under a minute, and it's safe to close
    the window: the claim keeps running.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="A claim runs in three steps, and you can close the dialog">
  <img src="/images/v3/faucet-01-claiming.png" alt="The Faucet with a claim in progress, showing Processing done, Confirming running with a link to the transaction, and Confirmed still to come" />
</Frame>

The faucet refuses a claim when:

- your wallet already holds **10,000** or more of that token
- a claim for the same token is still in progress
- the faucet has had repeated failures on that chain; try again later

## Gas

The faucet doesn't mint gas, but the **Gas** card on the Faucet page links
to where you get it:

- **Sepolia ETH** for your Ethereum wallet, from a public faucet. If one
  is dry, try the next:
  - [sepolia-faucet.pk910.de](https://sepolia-faucet.pk910.de/) needs no
    mainnet ETH: you mine in your browser to earn it.
  - [Alchemy Sepolia faucet](https://www.alchemy.com/faucets/ethereum-sepolia)
  - [GetBlock Sepolia faucet](https://getblock.io/faucet/eth-sepolia/)
  - [Chainlink Sepolia faucet](https://faucets.chain.link/sepolia)

  Most of the others ask you to sign in or hold a little mainnet ETH. The
  card has a copy button for your Ethereum address.
- **Testnet XLM** for your Stellar wallet: click **Use Friendbot** on the
  card. Friendbot opens and funds a new testnet account, free and
  instantly. It only funds new accounts.

<Note>
Key registration gas is paid by the relayer on both chains. You only need
gas for your own deposits, locks and ad transactions.
</Note>

### Gas for a hosted pod

A pod pays the fee for every lock from its own Ethereum and Stellar accounts,
not from your wallet. Fund them on the pod's **Fund your agent's gas** step,
or later from its **Machine** tab:

- **Top up ETH / Top up XLM** sends from your connected wallet.
- **Use Friendbot** funds the pod's Stellar account on testnet, with no XLM of
  your own.
- For test ETH, use a Sepolia faucet above and send it to the pod's Ethereum
  address shown on the card (it has a copy button).

See [Hosted pods](/v3/guides/hosted-pods) for the full setup.
