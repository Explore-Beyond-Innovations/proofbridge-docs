---
title: "Ads"
description: "Browse ads, and create, fund, withdraw from, pause and close your own"
---

An ad is a maker's standing offer on one route: funds escrowed in the `AdManager` on the ad chain,
paid out to bridgers who pay the order token on the other chain. These endpoints let anyone browse
ads and let a maker prepare every change to their own. See [Liquidity ads](/v3/concepts/liquidity-ads)
for the concept.

## Endpoints

| Endpoint | Auth | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `GET /v1/ads` | none | Browse ads, filtered and cursor-paginated |
| `GET /v1/ads/mine` | user | Your own ads, including inactive and closed ones |
| `GET /v1/ads/:id` | none | One ad |
| `POST /v1/ads/create` | user | Get the `createAd` call for a new ad |
| `POST /v1/ads/:id/fund` | user | Get the `fundAd` call to add to the pool |
| `POST /v1/ads/:id/withdraw` | user | Get the `withdrawFromAd` call to take funds out |
| `POST /v1/ads/:id/action-data` | user | Fetch the pending create or fund call again |
| `POST /v1/ads/:id/confirm` | user | Record that a create, fund, withdraw or close landed |
| `PATCH /v1/ads/:id/update` | user | Change limits and metadata, or pause and resume |
| `POST /v1/ads/:id/close` | user | Get the `closeAd` call to close the ad and pay out its balance |

## Who may act on an ad

Every write on an ad passes the same check:

1. You must have a signed-in wallet of the ad chain's kind (`EVM` or `STELLAR`). Otherwise the
   relayer answers `403` with `No STELLAR wallet linked — connect and link the matching wallet to continue`
   (or `EVM`).
2. If the ad's creator is that wallet, you may act (`creatorKind: "WALLET"`).
3. Otherwise the creator must be an account you linked (a Stellar agent account, or an EVM custody
   account, see [Accounts](/v3/api/accounts)), and the chain must say right now that its owner is
   your signed-in wallet. The owner is read from the chain on every write.

| Status | Message | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `403` | `Unauthorized` | The creator is not your wallet and not an account you linked |
| `403` | `Unauthorized — the account's on-chain owner is no longer your signed-in wallet` | The linked account changed owner |
| `503` | `The account's owner could not be read from the chain — retry` | The chain did not answer |

`GET /v1/ads/mine` uses a lighter rule: your stored wallets and links, with no chain read.

### Agent-account makers

A maker can run an ad through an agent account instead of a wallet (see [Agents](/v3/concepts/agents)).
Then `creatorKind` is `STELLAR_AGENT_ACCOUNT` or `EVM_CUSTODY_ACCOUNT`, `creator` is the account and
`authorizer` is its owner, your signed-in wallet. The wallet calls the account's own functions rather
than the `AdManager` directly: `create_ad`, `fund_ad` and `call` on Stellar, `createAdFunded`,
`fundAdFunded` and `execute` on EVM. Calls that move money pay from the wallet into the escrow in the
same transaction. The account never holds funds.

## Ad statuses

| Status | Meaning | Takes trades |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `INACTIVE` | Created by `POST /v1/ads/create`; the create transaction is not confirmed yet | no |
| `ACTIVE` | Open on chain and taking trades | yes, while `availableAmount` covers the amount and no change is pending |
| `PAUSED` | You paused it, or a fund, withdraw or close is pending | no |
| `EXHAUSTED` | Nothing is free to trade | no |
| `CLOSED` | Closed on chain, or a create that never landed | no |

The read endpoints show `EXHAUSTED` for any ad that is not `INACTIVE` or `CLOSED` and has
`availableAmount` `0`. The `status` filter, and the trade path, use the stored status.

A fund, withdraw or close on an `ACTIVE` ad pauses it at once, so no trade starts against a pool
that is about to change. When the change confirms, the ad goes back to its earlier status (or to
`EXHAUSTED` after a withdraw that leaves nothing free, or `CLOSED` after a close). If the change never
lands, the relayer restores the earlier status. A create that never lands ends as `CLOSED`.

## What the relayer returns, and what the wallet sends

The relayer signs nothing for an ad. Each write returns the contract call's arguments, shaped for the
ad chain. Your wallet builds and sends the transaction, and the relayer checks the chain afterwards.

| Action | EVM `AdManager` | Stellar `ad-manager` |
| --- | --- | --- |
| create | `createAd(adId, adToken, initialAmount, orderChainId, adRecipient, settlementSigner)`. `NATIVE`: send `initialAmount` as value. `ERC20`: approve the `AdManager` first | `create_ad(creator, ad_id, ad_token, initial_amount, order_chain_id, ad_recipient, settlement_signer)` |
| fund | `fundAd(adId, amount)` | `fund_ad(ad_id, amount)` |
| withdraw | `withdrawFromAd(adId, amount, to)` | `withdraw_from_ad(ad_id, amount, to)` |
| close | `closeAd(adId, to)`, pays the whole balance to `to` | `close_ad(ad_id, to)` |

`adId` is a UUID the relayer picks at create. It is both the relayer's id and the on-chain ad id.

### The settlement signer

Every ad names a **settlement signer**: the account whose BLS key co-signs settlements for the maker
(see [Settlement keys](/v3/concepts/settlement-keys)). It defaults to the creator. An agent account
holds no key, so an ad created from one must name a signer. The signer must hold a usable key on the
ad chain's registry when you create the ad, or the `AdManager` refuses it on chain. To re-point it
later, the maker calls `setSettlementSigner(adId, identity)` (`set_settlement_signer` on Stellar)
directly; there is no relayer route for it.

## Browse ads

`GET /v1/ads` lists ads, ordered by id. Pass `nextCursor` as `cursor` for the next page.

```json
// query parameters, all optional
{
  "routeId": "2f3a4b5c-6d7e-4f80-9a1b-2c3d4e5f6a7b",
  "creatorAddress": "0x8f14…6078",        // or creatorAddresses: a list, repeated or comma-separated
  "adChainId": 11155111,
  "orderChainId": 1000002,
  "adTokenId": "7e8f9a0b-…",
  "orderTokenId": "9c0d1e2f-…",
  "status": "ACTIVE",                      // ACTIVE | PAUSED | EXHAUSTED | CLOSED, the stored status
  "routeStatus": "ACTIVE",                 // ACTIVE | RETIRED | ALL
  "automated": true,                       // only ads that answer by themselves (true), or only the others (false)
  "q": "star",                             // 2 to 64 chars, in the title or the maker address, any case
  "sort": "automated-first",               // automated ads first; default is by id
  "cursor": "…",
  "limit": 25                              // 1 to 100; anything else means 25
}
```

- `routeStatus` defaults to `ACTIVE` when you browse, so ads on retired routes are hidden. With a
  creator filter it defaults to `ALL`, so a maker still sees an ad on a retired route to withdraw
  from and close it.
- With no `status`, inactive and closed ads are listed too. A marketplace should pass
  `status=ACTIVE` and hide rows whose `availableAmount` is `0`.
- An ad is **automated** when a live [hosted pod](/v3/api/pods) serves it, or a ProofBridge maker
  made it. `sort=automated-first` lists those first and is ignored when `automated` is set. Its
  cursors are its own: do not mix them with the default order's.
- `q` matches `%` and `_` as themselves. A whole Stellar `G…` address matches its stored form.

```json
{
  "data": [
    {
      "id": "b693ab22-5e73-47e8-9937-1d4459b8c081",
      "creatorAddress": "0x8f148F7aCCf2A2E6F21426741610619aF5856078",
      "creatorKind": "WALLET",                 // WALLET | STELLAR_AGENT_ACCOUNT | EVM_CUSTODY_ACCOUNT
      "routeId": "2f3a4b5c-6d7e-4f80-9a1b-2c3d4e5f6a7b",
      "adTokenId": "7e8f9a0b-1c2d-4e3f-9a4b-5c6d7e8f9a0b",
      "orderTokenId": "9c0d1e2f-3a4b-4c5d-8e6f-7a8b9c0d1e2f",
      "poolAmount": "5000000000000000000",     // the escrowed balance, smallest unit
      "availableAmount": "4000000000000000000",// poolAmount less every open trade hold
      "minAmount": "100000000000000000",
      "maxAmount": "2000000000000000000",
      "status": "ACTIVE",
      "routeStatus": "ACTIVE",
      "metadata": { "title": "ETH to Stellar, fast" },
      "servedByPod": false,                    // a live pod has this ad in its set
      "lastSettledAt": "2026-09-21T14:20:00.000Z", // when a trade on it last settled; null if none has
      "adToken": { "name": "Ether", "symbol": "ETH", "address": "0xeeee…eeee", "decimals": 18, "chainId": "11155111", "chainKind": "EVM", "kind": "NATIVE", "assetIssuer": null },
      "orderToken": { "name": "Wrapped Ether (Stellar)", "symbol": "ETH", "address": "0x4d5e…2b3c", "decimals": 18, "chainId": "1000002", "chainKind": "STELLAR", "kind": "SEP41", "assetIssuer": null },
      "createdAt": "2026-09-20T09:00:00.000Z",
      "updatedAt": "2026-09-21T14:30:00.000Z"
    }
  ],
  "nextCursor": null
}
```

| Status | Error | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `400` | validation | A query field is invalid, for example `routeId` is not a UUID, `status` is unknown, or `q` is under 2 characters |

## My ads

`GET /v1/ads/mine` takes the same query and returns the same shape, limited to ads whose creator is
one of your wallets or linked accounts. Any creator filter you send is replaced. This is where you see
your own `INACTIVE` and `CLOSED` ads, and ads on retired routes (send `routeStatus=ACTIVE` to hide
them). With no wallet and no link you get `{ "data": [], "nextCursor": null }`.

## One ad

`GET /v1/ads/:id` returns one ad in the same shape as a list row. An ad on a retired route still
answers, with `routeStatus: "RETIRED"`: its maker can fund, withdraw and close it, but it takes no new
trades.

| Status | Error | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `400` | `Validation failed (uuid is expected)` | `:id` is not a UUID |
| `404` | `Ad not found` | No ad with that id |

## Create an ad

`POST /v1/ads/create` checks the route, the amounts, the creator and the settlement signer, writes the
ad as `INACTIVE` and returns the `createAd` call.

```json
{
  "routeId": "2f3a4b5c-6d7e-4f80-9a1b-2c3d4e5f6a7b",
  "creatorDstAddress": "GBRPYHIL2CI3FNQ4BXLFMNDLFJUNPU2HY3ZMFSHONUCEOASW7QC7OX2H", // where you are paid, on the ORDER chain
  "fundAmount": "5000000000000000000",   // first deposit, smallest ad-token unit
  "minAmount": "100000000000000000",     // optional; send with maxAmount
  "maxAmount": "2000000000000000000",    // optional; at most fundAmount
  "metadata": { "title": "ETH to Stellar, fast" },
  "creator": "C…",                        // optional; default: your wallet on the ad chain
  "settlementSigner": "G…"                // optional; default: the creator. Required for an agent account
}
```

`creatorDstAddress` is a payout destination, not an identity, so it need not be a wallet you linked.
Send `minAmount` and `maxAmount` together or not at all: `minAmount` alone is refused.

```json
{
  "chainId": "11155111",                 // the ad chain
  "contractAddress": "0x3B2f…3A4b",      // the AdManager to call
  "adId": "b693ab22-5e73-47e8-9937-1d4459b8c081",
  "adToken": "0xeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeee",
  "initialAmount": "5000000000000000000",
  "orderChainId": "1000002",
  "adRecipient": "0x62fc…05f7",          // bytes32: creatorDstAddress, left-padded
  "settlementSigner": "0x0000…6078",     // bytes32
  "chainKind": "EVM",
  "creator": "0x8f148F7aCCf2A2E6F21426741610619aF5856078",
  "authorizer": "0x8f148F7aCCf2A2E6F21426741610619aF5856078", // the wallet that signs
  "creatorKind": "WALLET"
}
```

Send the call, then [confirm it](#confirm-a-landed-transaction).

| Status | Error | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `400` | validation | An unknown key, a bad UUID, or an amount that is not all digits |
| `404` | `Route not found` | The route does not exist |
| `409` | `route-retired` | The route is retired and takes no new ads |
| `400` | `Route must be cross-chain` | Both tokens are on one chain |
| `400` | `fundAmount must be > 0` | `fundAmount` is `0` |
| `400` | `maxAmount must be greater than minAmount` | `maxAmount` is below `minAmount`, or `minAmount` came without `maxAmount` |
| `400` | `fundAmount must be greater than maxAmount` | `fundAmount` is below `maxAmount` |
| `400` | `Invalid creatorDstAddress` | Not an address on the order chain, or the zero address |
| `400` | `creator is not an address on the ad chain` | Bad `creator` |
| `403` / `503` | | The [control check](#who-may-act-on-an-ad) refused the creator |
| `400` | `This creator is a contract account and holds no key: …` | An agent account with no `settlementSigner` |
| `400` | `settlementSigner must be an EVM address, a 32-byte hex or a Stellar G… key` | Bad `settlementSigner` |
| `400` | `Register a settlement key on the ad chain before creating an ad …` | The signer has no usable key |
| `400` | `The agent policy bound to this creator names settlement signer …` | An agent policy names a different signer |
| `503` | `Key registry unreachable — retry` | The key registry could not be read |

## Fund and withdraw

`POST /v1/ads/:id/fund` and `POST /v1/ads/:id/withdraw` read the ad on chain, record the pending
change, pause an `ACTIVE` ad and return the call.

```json
// fund
{ "poolAmountTopUp": "1000000000000000000" }

// withdraw
{
  "poolAmountWithdraw": "1000000000000000000",
  "to": "0x8f148f7accf2a2e6f21426741610619af5856078"  // on the AD chain; Stellar: a G… or C… strkey
}
```

```json
{
  "chainId": "1000002",
  "contractAddress": "0x8c0f…ccdd",
  "adId": "0d4c3b2a-1f0e-4d9c-8b7a-6f5e4d3c2b1a",
  "amount": "50000000",
  "to": "0x62fc…05f7",                    // withdraw only; 0x-64 on Stellar
  "toStrkey": "GBRPYHIL2CI3FNQ4BXLFMNDLFJUNPU2HY3ZMFSHONUCEOASW7QC7OX2H", // Stellar withdraw only
  "chainKind": "STELLAR",
  "creator": "CDAZH3BGNLL5VIK24OB2SSBAHFWI2ITUW24EQYJ3DY5I4IZIMTOOH6QJ",
  "authorizer": "GBRPYHIL2CI3FNQ4BXLFMNDLFJUNPU2HY3ZMFSHONUCEOASW7QC7OX2H",
  "creatorKind": "STELLAR_AGENT_ACCOUNT"
}
```

You can withdraw only what is free on chain: the balance, less locked amounts, less trade
reservations not yet locked.

Only one change can be pending per ad. If an earlier change already landed but was never confirmed,
the answer is `{ "pendingConfirmation": true, "action": "FUND", "chainKind": "EVM" }` (`action` is
`WITHDRAW` on withdraw): call [confirm](#confirm-a-landed-transaction), and the request is done.

| Status | Error | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `404` | `Ad not found` | No such ad |
| `403` / `503` | | The [control check](#who-may-act-on-an-ad) refused |
| `409` | `ad-closed` | The ad is `CLOSED` |
| `409` | `ad-update-awaiting-confirmations` | An earlier change landed but is not deep enough yet |
| `400` | `This ad's last change hasn't finished; try again in <n> minutes` | An earlier create or close is still pending, or one the chain can't check yet. A fund or withdraw that never landed is replaced at once |
| `400` | `poolAmountTopUp must be > 0` / `poolAmountWithdraw must be > 0` | Amount is `0` |
| `400` | `Insufficient available balance` | Withdraw is more than the free amount |
| `400` | `Invalid withdraw destination (Stellar: a G… or C… strkey)` | Bad `to` |
| `400` | `Ad not found on chain` | The ad is not on chain |
| `503` | `Ad chain unreadable — retry` | The ad chain could not be read |

## Fetch the pending call again

`POST /v1/ads/:id/action-data` re-serves the arguments of a pending **create** or **fund**, for a
wallet popup that was closed. It takes no body and makes no new pending record. A pending withdraw or
close is not re-served: wait for it to clear and start again.

```json
{
  "action": "fund",                       // create | fund
  "chainId": "11155111",
  "contractAddress": "0x3B2f…3A4b",
  "adId": "b693ab22-5e73-47e8-9937-1d4459b8c081",
  "amount": "1000000000000000000",        // fund only; a create returns the createAd fields instead
  "creator": "0x8f148F7aCCf2A2E6F21426741610619aF5856078",
  "authorizer": "0x8f148F7aCCf2A2E6F21426741610619aF5856078",
  "creatorKind": "WALLET",
  "chainKind": "EVM"
}
```

It does not check whether the call already landed, and sending a landed create again reverts. If the
user may have signed, call confirm first.

| Status | Error | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `404` | `No pending action for this ad` | Nothing is pending |
| `403` / `503` | | The [control check](#who-may-act-on-an-ad) refused |
| `409` | `This pending action cannot be re-served — wait for it to clear, then re-initiate` | The pending action is a withdraw or close |

## Confirm a landed transaction

`POST /v1/ads/:id/confirm` checks the chain against the pending change. The relayer does not look up
your transaction: `txHash` is optional and not read, so `{}` is a valid body.

```json
{ "txHash": "0x123f…8a7e" }   // optional
```

```json
{ "adId": "b693ab22-5e73-47e8-9937-1d4459b8c081", "success": true }
```

A create has landed when the ad exists on chain with the right maker, signer and balance. A fund or
withdraw has landed when the `AdManager`'s own event for this ad and amount appears after the quote;
on EVM it must be `EVM_CONFIRMATIONS` blocks deep. A close has landed when the ad is no longer open. On
confirm the relayer copies the chain's balance into `poolAmount` and applies the recorded status.

If a create landed after the relayer gave up on it and closed the row, confirm still finds it on chain
and restores the ad to `ACTIVE`.

| Status | Error | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `409` | `ad-update-awaiting-confirmations` | The event is not deep enough; `confirmationsRemaining` says how many blocks to wait |
| `409` | `ad-update-unverifiable` | The chain's RPC can no longer serve every block since the quote; the relayer clears the change and re-reads the pool |
| `400` | `ad-update-not-landed` | No matching event after the quote; start the action again |
| `404` | `Ad update log not found` | Nothing is pending and there is no late create to restore |
| `403` / `503` | | The [control check](#who-may-act-on-an-ad) refused |
| `503` | `Ad chain unreadable — retry` | The ad chain could not be read |

A change that is never confirmed is not lost. The relayer checks stale pending changes on chain and
applies or clears them itself.

## Update limits, metadata or pause

`PATCH /v1/ads/:id/update` changes relayer-side settings. There is no transaction and the change takes
effect at once. The on-chain ad has no minimum, maximum or pause; these settings shape what the relayer
offers bridgers.

```json
{
  "status": "PAUSED",                     // optional: ACTIVE | PAUSED
  "minAmount": "100000000000000000",      // optional
  "maxAmount": "3000000000000000000",     // optional
  "metadata": { "title": "ETH to Stellar, fast" } // optional; replaces the whole object
}
```

```json
{
  "id": "b693ab22-5e73-47e8-9937-1d4459b8c081",
  "status": "PAUSED",
  "creatorAddress": "0x8f148F7aCCf2A2E6F21426741610619aF5856078",
  "minAmount": "100000000000000000",
  "maxAmount": "3000000000000000000",
  "metadata": { "title": "ETH to Stellar, fast" }
}
```

Limits and metadata are editable in every status. A `status` change is refused on an `INACTIVE` or
`CLOSED` ad. On an `EXHAUSTED` ad the relayer reads the chain and allows it only when funds are free
there. The relayer does not check `minAmount ≤ maxAmount` here, so check it in your form, and offer
pause and resume only on `ACTIVE` or `PAUSED` ads with no pending change.

| Status | Error | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `400` | validation | For example `status: "CLOSED"` |
| `404` | `Ad not found` | No such ad |
| `403` / `503` | | The [control check](#who-may-act-on-an-ad) refused |
| `400` | `Cannot update status when ad is inactive` | `status` on an `INACTIVE` ad |
| `409` | `ad-closed` | `status` on a `CLOSED` ad |
| `409` | `ad-exhausted` | `status` on an `EXHAUSTED` ad with nothing free; fund it first |
| `409` | `ad-update-pending` | `status` while a top-up, withdrawal or close on the ad hasn't finished. Finish it, or try again in the minutes the message names |
| `503` | `Ad chain unreadable — retry` | `status` on an `EXHAUSTED` ad and the chain did not answer |

## Close an ad

`POST /v1/ads/:id/close` returns the `closeAd` call, which pays the whole on-chain balance to `to`.
Close is refused while any trade holds liquidity on the ad, locked on chain or reserved.

```json
{ "to": "0x8f148f7accf2a2e6f21426741610619af5856078" }  // on the AD chain; Stellar: a G… or C… strkey
```

```json
{
  "chainId": "11155111",
  "contractAddress": "0x3B2f…3A4b",
  "adId": "b693ab22-5e73-47e8-9937-1d4459b8c081",
  "to": "0x8f148F7aCCf2A2E6F21426741610619aF5856078",
  "chainKind": "EVM",
  "creator": "0x8f148F7aCCf2A2E6F21426741610619aF5856078",
  "authorizer": "0x8f148F7aCCf2A2E6F21426741610619aF5856078",
  "creatorKind": "WALLET"
}
```

Like fund and withdraw, it can answer `{ "pendingConfirmation": true, "action": "CLOSE", … }` when an
earlier close landed unconfirmed, and it shares their pending-change and chain refusals.

| Status | Error | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `400` | `Ad is already closed` | Already `CLOSED` |
| `400` | `Ad has active locks and cannot be closed` | A trade holds liquidity |
| `400` | `Invalid close destination (Stellar: a G… or C… strkey)` | Bad `to` |

## Liquidity held by trades

When a bridger [creates a trade](/v3/api/trades#create-a-trade), the relayer holds that amount of the
ad. `availableAmount` drops by it, a withdraw cannot take it, and close is refused. The hold ends when
the trade settles or is cancelled, or when an unfunded reservation expires after 30 minutes. Because
the relayer copies the chain's balance after each settlement, `poolAmount` can change without any
action from you: a completed trade lowers it.
