---
title: "Deadlines and clocks"
description: "Every order has a deadline. How the two chains agree on when a trade must settle and how it ends"
---

Every order carries a **deadline**: the time by which the trade has to
settle. The deadline is part of the [order](/v3/concepts/order-hashing),
so both chains and both parties see the same one.

## One chain decides, the other follows

Two chains can't share a clock. ProofBridge gives the decision to one of
them:

- The **ad chain** (`AdManager`) is the **leader**. Its clock alone decides
  how a trade ends: settled, cancelled or ruled on.
- The **order chain** (`OrderPortal`) is the **follower**. It has no clock
  of its own for endings. It acts on a proof of what the ad chain
  decided: a settled payout, a cancel or a forfeit.

This way the two chains can never decide differently about the same
trade.

## The route's clocks

Each route sets four timings. They're fixed for an order when it's created
and locked, so a later change never affects a trade in flight.

| Timing | What it does | Soak value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shortest order window | the least time between creating an order and its deadline | 20 minutes |
| Cancel window | after the deadline, how long before a started cancel can complete | 30 minutes |
| Margin | how long before the end of the cancel window the ad chain stops accepting a co-signed payout | 5 minutes |
| Claim stagger | how long before the deadline the order chain stops accepting a co-signed payout | 1 minute |

The relayer sets an order's deadline when the order is created. It takes
the larger of two lengths: its own configured deadline, or twice the
route's shortest window (so the maker's lock still lands in time). It
never goes past the escrows' 7-day limit. The relayer's default is one
day; soak sets it to 2 hours, so a soak order's deadline is about 2 hours
after you create it.

## How the timeline plays out

1. **Before the deadline**, the trade settles normally. The order chain
   stops accepting the co-signed payout one claim stagger before the
   deadline.
2. **At the deadline**, anyone can start a cancel on the ad chain. That
   opens the cancel window.
3. **During the cancel window**, a co-signed payout on the ad chain, or a
   proof that the trade settled, still ends the trade as settled. Either
   party can also file a [dispute](/v3/concepts/disputes).
4. **After the window**, the cancel completes. The Maker's lock returns
   to the ad, and the Bridger's deposit is refunded on the order chain.
   See [Cancels and refunds](/v3/concepts/cancels-and-refunds).

## When a payout was blocked

If a co-signed payout couldn't go through, for example because the Maker
halted settlement or the pinned key ended, the cancel waits a little
longer. That extra time lets the Bridger present evidence that the trade
should settle, so a Maker can't block the payout and then cancel.

## Pausing doesn't move deadlines

A pause stops only new activity: new ads, funding, locks and orders.
Every ending stays open, so pausing never stretches or shortens a window.
