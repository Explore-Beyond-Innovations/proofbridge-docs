ProofBridge settles trades between **Ethereum** and **Stellar**. Each
chain runs the same set of contracts, and every route works in both
directions.

## Networks

  <Card title="Stellar Testnet" icon="/logos/stellar.svg">

    Soroban contracts. Supports native XLM, Stellar Asset Contracts (SAC)

<Note>
Stellar has no numeric chain ID of its own. ProofBridge gives each
Stellar network a fixed ID so orders can name both chains the same way.
</Note>

## Routes

Tokens pair across chains by value: what you deposit on one chain, you
receive on the other, less the [bridge fee](/v3/concepts/fees).
Amounts are aligned across different token decimals automatically
([decimal scaling](/v3/concepts/decimal-scaling)).

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="ETH ↔ wETH">
    | Direction | You deposit | You receive |
    |---|---|---|
    | Sepolia → Stellar | ETH (wrapped on deposit) | wETH (SEP-41) |
    | Stellar → Sepolia | wETH (SEP-41) | ETH (unwrapped on payout) |
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="XLM ↔ WXLM">
    | Direction | You deposit | You receive |
    |---|---|---|
    | Stellar → Sepolia | XLM (wrapped on deposit) | WXLM (ERC-20) |
    | Sepolia → Stellar | WXLM (ERC-20) | XLM (unwrapped on payout) |
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="PB">
    | Direction | You deposit | You receive |
    |---|---|---|
    | Sepolia → Stellar | PB (ERC-20) | PB (SEP-41) |
    | Stellar → Sepolia | PB (SEP-41) | PB (ERC-20) |

    PB is a test token for trying the flow end to end.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Token addresses are on the [Smart contracts](/v3/reference/smart-contracts)
page.

## No sign-up

You connect with your wallet. There are no accounts, usernames or KYC.
Your wallet address is your identity, and the trade terms are enforced
by the contracts on each chain.
