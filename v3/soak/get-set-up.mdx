## 1. Wallets

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Ethereum Sepolia" icon="/logos/ethereum.svg">
    MetaMask, Rainbow or another browser wallet on desktop. Works on a phone too, through
    WalletConnect or your wallet's own browser.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Stellar Testnet" icon="/logos/stellar.svg">
    Freighter, Lobstr, xBull or Hana on desktop. On a phone, Freighter Mobile over WalletConnect is
    worth trying, but it's untested. See the note below.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Warning>
  **No Stellar phone wallet is verified yet.** A settlement key needs the wallet to sign a
  message. The app to try is **Freighter Mobile** over WalletConnect: it supports message
  signing, but no signature from it has been checked here yet. Set it to **Testnet** before you
  scan, or it declines the pairing. Lobstr pairs over WalletConnect, but its session does not
  permit message signing.

  You can still test on a phone with your **Ethereum wallet**. Sign in on Sepolia,
  register your settlement key, and type your Stellar address when you bridge, so no Stellar
  wallet is needed to be paid. Anything that signs on the Stellar side, such as posting an ad
  or trading from a Stellar wallet, is surest in a desktop browser.
</Warning>

Switch each wallet to its testnet: **Sepolia** in your EVM wallet, **Testnet** in your Stellar wallet.

Open [staging.pfbridge.xyz](https://staging.pfbridge.xyz), click **Connect Wallet**, and sign in on each
network. Connecting the second wallet links it to the same account. [Connect a wallet](/v3/guides/connect-wallet).

## 2. Test funds

| You need | Where |
| --- | --- |

| Testnet XLM, for fees | Stellar Friendbot (it also creates a new account) |
| The bridge tokens (WXLM and PB on Sepolia, wETH and PB on Stellar) | the in-app **Faucet**: 1,000,000 per claim |

## 3. Your settlement key

Every wallet you trade from needs a settlement key, registered once. On the **BLS Keys** page, click **Register**,
sign the derivation message, then sign the registration. The relayer pays the gas.
[Register a settlement key](/v3/guides/register-a-settlement-key).

<Frame caption="Pick the wallet to generate the key from, then sign twice">
  <img src="/images/v3/soak-04-register-key.png" alt="The BLS Signing Key dialog with Ethereum Sepolia and Stellar Testnet to choose between, and a Generate Key button" />
</Frame>

One wallet at a time. After the first, the counters read **01 ready, 01 needs registration** —
that second one is your other wallet, so repeat for it.

<Frame caption="First key done. The other wallet still says Not Registered">
  <img src="/images/v3/soak-05-first-key-done.png" alt="Registration Complete dialog, with one address Registered and the other still Not Registered behind it" />
</Frame>

While you're there, **file an emergency stop** for your key: it's part of what we're testing.
